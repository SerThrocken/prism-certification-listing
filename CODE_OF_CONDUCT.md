**Thomas Lincoln Crockett / TLG3D Labs, LLC** — github.com/SerThrocken

*Stub, prepared for publication with the listing repo (task B15). Thomas approves the final text at publication; this stub fixes the commitments the public repo will state, in short form.*

## The short version

This repository is a public record of evidence. Behaviour here is judged by one standard: does it keep the record honest and the discussion usable by the people who rely on it?

- Argue with evidence. A claim about a result links its result file; a disagreement about a suite names the case.
- Attack packs, test cases, and breaks are welcome — attacking people is not. Credit for a break goes to its author (§11.9), including when the break embarrasses this project.
- No harassment, no threats, no publishing another person's private information, no impersonation of a bench key holder, witness, or maintainer.
- Disclose conflicts: if you maintain a runtime or pack with rows in this listing, say so when you comment on its rows or on a suite that tests it.

## Enforcement

Reports go through the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (marked "conduct" if the report is about behaviour rather than a break). Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and — for repeated or severe breaches — block participation in this repository. Enforcement actions that affect the public record itself (a removed proposal, a struck comment on a §11.1 proposal) are noted in `governance/` with the reason, because silent edits to a governance record are exactly what this project exists to prevent.

## Scope

This code applies in this repository's issues, discussions, pull requests, and proposal comment windows, and to conduct in project spaces that directly affects the listing's record.
